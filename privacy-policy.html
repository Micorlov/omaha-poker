Privacy Policy for Omaha Poker: Friends & Points

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Short version: the game does not ask you to create an account, and it does not collect your name, email address, or phone number. An anonymous install id is created so the app can deliver notifications and store a few stats. The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Depending on your consent choices, AdMob may use the advertising ID to personalise ads. We do not sell your data.

Omaha Poker: Friends & Points ("we", "us") is a free-to-play poker game using virtual points with no cash value. This policy explains what the Android app with package name com.omaha.omaha_poker does with your data.

Information We Collect

The app does not offer Google sign-in and does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. It does not request a location permission.

When the device is online, the app sends the following to Google Firebase, under an anonymous install id created on the device:

  • Install record: anonymous Firebase user id, app version, platform, the device locale and its country code, timezone offset, notification preferences, and the last time the app was opened.
  • Push token: the Firebase Cloud Messaging token for this install, so we can send the notifications you leave enabled.
  • Gameplay counters: hands played and won, virtual-chip balance and lifetime net, biggest pot, best hand category, play time, and the UTC date of the last hand. These are totals, not a card-by-card history.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics for Firebase receives the screen you are on (for example lobby or table), tables joined and left, completed hands, the first-hands coach, level, achievements, virtual chips earned or spent outside a hand, Sit & Go results, the out-of-chips prompt, and notification taps. Your player level is stored as an analytics user property.

Data Stored On Your Device

A larger copy of your game stays in the app's private storage and is not uploaded as a history:

  • Gameplay data: your virtual points balance, hand history, level, and statistics such as hands played and win rate.
  • Friends list: names and scores you add locally. Adding a friend does not contact that person and does not sync to their device.
  • Preferences: settings such as sound, language, and text size.

Advertising

This app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner along the bottom of the screen, and an occasional full-screen ad between hands. There are no in-app purchases. Virtual points cannot be bought, sold, cashed out, or exchanged for anything of value.

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. In these regions the app shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) on launch. Until you answer, ads are not requested, and Google Analytics for Firebase stays off (consent signals analytics_storage, ad_storage, ad_user_data and ad_personalization are set to denied). After you answer, those signals follow your choices. Elsewhere, where no consent form is required, analytics are on by default and ads are requested normally. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Profile shows an Ad privacy row where you can change your choices at any time.

AdMob may collect and use device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID, and data about which ads are shown and tapped. Where the law requires a choice, the app asks before requesting ads, using Google's consent form. In those regions, Profile includes an Ad privacy row that reopens that form. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings. What Google does with ad data is described in Google's advertising policy. AdMob may infer an approximate location from the IP address; the app itself does not read GPS.

Third Parties

We do not sell or rent personal data. The services above are Google's:

  • Firebase Authentication (anonymous install id only), Cloud Firestore (the install record), Cloud Messaging, and Google Analytics for Firebase.
  • Google AdMob, as described under Advertising.

Children's Privacy

This game features simulated gambling and is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad requests are not tagged for child or teen treatment, and ads are limited to content suitable for a Teen rating.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR), you may ask us to access, correct or delete the data we hold about your install, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time (use the Ad privacy row in Profile, or email us). You may also complain to your local data protection authority. Our legal bases are your consent for analytics and personalised ads, and our legitimate interest in running the game's notifications and stats.

Your Control Over Your Data

You can remove the on-device data at any time:

  • Go to Android Settings → Apps → Omaha Poker: Friends & Points → Storage → Clear data, or
  • Uninstall the app, which deletes its local data, including the anonymous session.

Clearing data or uninstalling does not automatically delete the anonymous install record already stored in Firebase. To ask us to delete that record, email micorlov@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Omaha Poker data. There is no account email to match the request to, so include the approximate install date and country if you can. We will delete the install record we can tie to the request within 30 days. See also Delete your data.
